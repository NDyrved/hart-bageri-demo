The Bakery

Everything baked in-house

A small, focused menu — made carefully each day with simple ingredients and time-honoured technique.

Sourdough Bread

Slow-fermented loaves with a deep crust and open crumb. Baked daily and best enjoyed warm.

Pastries

Buttery, laminated layers and seasonal fillings. A small selection that changes through the week.

Croissants

Hand-rolled, properly proofed, and baked golden. Plain, chocolate, and almond — every morning.

Cakes

Layered, refined, and made to order. Whole cakes available daily, or by request for special occasions.

Coffee

About Hart Bageri

A neighbourhood bakery in Copenhagen V

That pursuit lives in the small things. The slow ferment of every sourdough loaf. The careful lamination of each croissant. The quiet attention behind every cup of coffee we pour.

Step inside and we hope it feels like a part of your day, not a stop along it. A loaf for dinner, a pastry between meetings, a cake for the weekend — made well, made here, made for the neighbourhood.
