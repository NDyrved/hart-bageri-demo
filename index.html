About Hart Bageri

A neighbourhood bakery in Copenhagen V

Hart Bageri is a small bakery built around a simple idea — good bread, made well, every day. We work with care, time, and a short list of trusted ingredients.

From morning sourdough to afternoon pastries and a quiet cup of coffee, we want the bakery to feel like a part of your day. Walk in, take your time, and leave with something good.
